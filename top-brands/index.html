Our Philosophy

A Symphony of Uncompromising Quality

Founded by Maître Glacier Jean-Luc Moreau, Maison de Glace approaches frozen dessert not as a treat, but as an elevated canvas of gastronomy. Every batch is spun in micro-quantities daily to achieve a velvety texture that defies conventional standards.

We source our pistachios exclusively from Bronte, Sicily, our vanilla beans from small-scale plantations in Madagascar, and our dairy fresh from organic pastures in Normandy.

The Difference

Crafted Without Compromise

01

Micro-Batch Spun

Produced exclusively in quantities under ten liters to maintain absolute control over texture, overrun, and temperature profiling.

02

100% Traceable

We work directly with generational farmers and ethical growers who share our uncompromising dedication to the earth and harvest.

03

Pure Composition

Free from artificial stabilizers, emulsifiers, and synthetic colorants. What you taste is simply the raw integrity of the ingredient.
